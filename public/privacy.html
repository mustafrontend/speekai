Privacy Policy

SpeekAI: AI Speech to Text & Voice Notes
Effective Date: August 11, 2026

SpeekAI ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by SpeekAI.

1. 100% Device Privacy & Zero Server Audio Storage

Your audio recordings and dictations are processed locally using your device's native Speech-to-Text framework. SpeekAI does not store, record, or upload your raw audio files to any external database servers.

2. Local Storage Persistence

All saved notes, transcripts, custom tags, and user preferences are saved strictly within your device's browser/application local storage (LocalStorage). You retain full control to delete your notes at any time.

3. Artificial Intelligence Processing (Google Gemini AI)

When you explicitly request AI features (such as To-Do Action Summaries or AI Tone Transformations), text transcripts are sent securely over HTTPS to the Google Gemini AI API solely for generating your requested summary. Google processes this text transiently and does not use your data to train public models.

4. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

All financial transactions, payments, and subscriptions are securely processed by Apple App Store Commerce Services and RevenueCat SDK. SpeekAI does not have access to your credit card details or billing credentials.

5. Information Collected Automatically

We collect minimal non-identifiable usage analytics (e.g., daily note limit counter) to enforce subscription tiers ($3.99/week or $29.99/year).
